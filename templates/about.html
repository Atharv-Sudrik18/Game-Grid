About Game Grid

Welcome to Game Grid, a Sports Tournament Management System.
Game Grid is designed to make the management of sports tournaments simple, organized, and efficient.

Objectives

1. Create and manage tournaments
2. Manage teams and players
3. Schedule matches
4. Update match results
5. View points tables
6. Track tournament progress
